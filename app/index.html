Add a music video to a chart you already have

The app finds where the chart's music is inside the video, cuts the video's longer intro (or puts black in front if the video starts later) and starts the picture with the music. The chart is backed up first as mstr.dtx.before_mv. If the video is a different edit (an extra scene or a cut in the middle), it lists the measures where the picture will be out of sync.

The app finds the score pages and the timing. Reading the notes from the pages into score.txt is still done by Claude, then press Generate again.

Opens a chart you already have: check and move notes, play it, and change the song info, jacket and preview start. "Save changes" writes into the same folder (the chart as it was is kept once as mstr.dtx.bak).
